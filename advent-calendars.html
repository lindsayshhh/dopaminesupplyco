Fun Advent Calendars for Adults, Kids and Pets

Rx: One small surprise daily, December 1 to 24

An advent calendar turns December into 24 tiny gift openings, and the best ones are built around something the person already loves. This guide has puzzle calendars that reveal a mystery one clue at a time, build-a-day sets that end as a bouquet or a tiny garden, beauty and snack calendars, one for the cat and a few for kids. The popular ones sell out by mid-November, so order early if you want it there by December 1.

Advent calendar questions.

When should you buy an advent calendar?

As early as you can. Popular advent calendars often sell out in November, and one only works if it arrives before December 1. Ordering by mid-November is the safe bet.

What day do you open the first door?

Most advent calendars start on December 1 and end on December 24. A 12-day calendar, like the Kitsch one here, can start on December 13 or any day you like.

What is a good advent calendar for adults?

Pick one built around something they already love. Puzzle fans get the detective mystery or the EXIT escape room, tabletop gamers get the D&D dice and home bartenders get the whiskey smoker kit.

Is it too late to give one after December 1?

Not at all. They can open the missed doors on day one and carry on from there. With the puzzle calendars, they can solve a few days in one sitting to catch up.

Are there advent calendars for pets?

Yes. The cat toy advent calendar has a different toy behind each of its 24 doors, so the cat gets a countdown too.
