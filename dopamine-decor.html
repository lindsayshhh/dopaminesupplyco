Dopamine Decor for a Happier Home

Rx: For a room that needs more joy

Dopamine decor means filling your space with color and things that make you happy, without worrying whether they match. Start with one bright piece, like a disco-ball planter or a rainbow maker for the window, and build from there. Everything here works in a bedroom, dorm or first apartment.
